A field guide to things that grow themselves

Specimen 0 · Bare ground

Nothing has been grown here yet. The guide has its first page and no specimens on it — no seeds planted, no rules written, nothing yet that could be stepped forward a generation to see what it becomes.

Specimens arrive one rule at a time. The first will stand on this page beside the rule that grew it, ready to be re-seeded, walked generation by generation, and asked whether its own properties still hold.
